How to play Ludo

  1. Four players each control four tokens.
  2. Roll a six to bring a token out of its base.
  3. Tap a highlighted token to move it clockwise.
  4. A six earns another turn.
  5. Land on an opponent on a capturable square to send that token back to base.
  6. Safe squares protect tokens from capture.
  7. Enter your own coloured home lane after completing the outer route.
  8. Reach the finish with an exact roll. The first player to get all four tokens home wins.

This is a simplified browser demo. Rule variations exist between physical Ludo editions.
